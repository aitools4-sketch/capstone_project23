[ 01 ]

The problem

Antivirus and firewalls guard the device in front of you — not the identity that leaks once your email and password sit inside someone else’s breached database. Most people never find out until the damage is done, because verifying exposure manually means combing through breach dumps built for security researchers, not everyday users.

[ 02 ]

Objectives

Turn breach exposure into one number you can act on.

— Identify exposure through the DeHashed API

— Score severity with Scikit-learn

— Explain findings through OpenAI

— Evaluate against ISO/IEC 25010

Methodology

Development approach

Agile SDLC, executed across six iterative phases from requirements analysis through maintenance.

Data gathering

Structured and semi-structured interviews with security administrators and system owners, alongside an ISO/IEC 25010 survey completed by end-users and IT personnel, safeguarded under RA 10173.

What this delivers
